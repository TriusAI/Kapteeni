Benchmarks & Calibration

All runs on this page are self-reported measurements on the public half of JevBench v1.4 — 231 items: easy 48 / standard 72 / hard 111 — using the benchmark's own official scoring code, through the live server, end to end.

Standing caveat

This is not an official JevBench rank. The judge tier (146 items) is sealed, the calibration axis's gold-distribution half is private, and the official score requires the 308 sealed items. Repo policy since 2026-09-26: report numbers and their caveats, and make no claims about the leaderboard or placement.

§ 6Run history

  1. 01

    v0 — heads on a frozen backbone

    Families matching the training data (fact, tool_selection, extraction) score 0.92–1.00; out-of-distribution families fall to 0.22–0.32. Top-label ECE 0.232 — heads overconfident exactly off-distribution.

    score 17.9 · acc 0.537
  2. 02

    Phase 1 — verbalizer hybrid

    The backbone's native decision readout blended with the heads: p ∝ p_head^w · exp((1−w)·s_verb/τ). The pre-registered w=0.7 scores 26.3; w=0.25 — selected on the public half, flagged — scores 41.2. Every step toward the verbalizer improves OOD Intelligence and calibration monotonically.

    score 41.2
  3. 03

    v0.1 — run of record

    Serving fix: pre-softmax scores passed to the contract shapers (the deployed blend had been softmaxed twice). p50 0.17 s / p95 1.19 s.

    score 48.21 · acc 0.615
  4. 04

    v1 — meticulous, the flagship

    LoRA r=32 on all projections + retrained heads over the 8.7M-token mix; MNLI held out as the OOD gate (0.88 → 0.893). All serving constants refit on mixed-domain val through the merged model — zero public-half selection in the chain.

    score 65.71 · acc 0.710
  5. 05

    v1.1 — three-seed soup

    Negative result, kept honest. Every mixed-val gate passed before the run; bench ECE then doubled (0.0496 → 0.1046). Demonstrates the val-fit-constants OOD failure mode; ensembling disqualified for this model+data until the mechanism is understood.

    score 63.09 · ECE 0.1046
  6. 06

    v1.2 — weak-family continuation

    Accuracy improved (0.723; Intelligence 62.0, best measured; hard 0.496) but the val-fit choice constants saturated probabilities to 1.0/0.0 and bench ECE hit 0.2023. The flagship gate failed decisively; v1 stays shipped.

    score 59.65 · ECE 0.2023
  7. 07

    v1.2.1 — diverse-constant refit, shipped as -intuit

    Pre-registered: freeze the seed-3 artifacts, refit temperatures + blend on the deployment-diverse val set (mixed 590 + synth2 609). Saturation eliminated, bench ECE halved vs v1.2; gate 3 failed (63.18 < 64.71), so the flagship stays -meticulous. Per pre-registration, the last change gated on public-half outcomes.

    score 63.18 · ECE 0.1196

Accumulated-exposure note, from the record: the last two redesigns were informed by bench outcomes; each was mechanism-driven and one-run-per-candidate, but the aggregate record is weakly bench-informed. The v1.2.1 protocol fix was therefore made the last bench-gated change. A design proposal for a deployment-oriented alternative lives in DECISIONBENCH-DRAFT.md.

§ 7How to read every number above

  • Sample sizes are small. All accuracies are on 231 items; one item is 0.43 accuracy points. 95% binomial CIs on public accuracy: v0.1 0.615 ± 6.3pt, v1 0.710 ± 5.9pt. Tier-level CIs are wider (hard: ±9.3pt at v1's 0.469).
  • Small composite gaps are ties. Differences of a few points between any two runs sit inside run-to-run variance plus axis assumptions (the Cost axis alone swings ±7 points across the stated price range).
  • A measured noise floor. v1 vs the soup differ only by training seed and val-fit constants and scored 65.71 vs 63.09 — treat ~±2–3 composite points as the single-seed pipeline noise floor.
  • ECE at n=231 is biased and high-variance. Differences below ~0.02 between single runs are unresolvable; the soup's 0.0496 → 0.1046 doubling is a real effect, but 0.05-vs-0.07 comparisons elsewhere should not be read as meaningful.
  • Calibration is measured on the benchmark's distribution, which is OOD relative to any deployment: a model whose constants are tuned for the bench mix is not thereby calibrated for yours. That failure mode is exactly how the soup passed every val gate while its bench ECE doubled.
  • Intelligence is renormalized over the three public tiers because the judge tier is sealed; any score that folds the sealed+judge weight in is not comparable to ours. Jev's accuracy on the same public items is still higher (0.866 vs 0.710).
